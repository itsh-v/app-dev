# My Favorite Movie: White Chicks

## About the Movie
**White Chicks** is a *comedy* film released in **2004** and directed by Keenen Ivory Wayans. It follows two FBI agents who go undercover in a very unexpected way.

## Plot Summary
> Two FBI agents, Kevin and Marcus Copeland, are assigned to protect two wealthy hotel heiresses. When the plan falls apart, they disguise themselves as the sisters to complete the mission.

## Main Characters
- **Kevin Copeland** (Shawn Wayans) - an FBI agent
- **Marcus Copeland** (Marlon Wayans) - an FBI agent and Kevin's brother
- **Brittany and Tiffany Wilson** - the heiresses they pretend to be
- **Latrell Spencer** (Terry Crews) - a funny and memorable supporting character

## Why I Like It
1. It is very funny and entertaining
2. The characters are unforgettable
3. It is perfect to watch with friends and family
4. The undercover story is creative
